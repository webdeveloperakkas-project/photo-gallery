GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A little more clarity before you take your next step.

What kind of digital assets can I explore?+

We are building for websites, content businesses, SaaS products, e-commerce stores, domains, creator assets and other digital properties.

Do I have to pay to list an asset?+

Our planned model includes free listings, with no monthly seller maintenance fee.

How does the transaction process work?+

Buyers and sellers discuss and agree on the asset terms. Payment is then handled through the supported transaction process, followed by asset transfer and buyer confirmation.

Are the assets shown here real listings?+

No. The cards on this preview homepage are illustrative design examples. Actual listings will be identified clearly when the marketplace is operational.
