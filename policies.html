Payment & Fee Policy

Nexa marketplace fee — 12% total

The standard Nexa marketplace fee on a successfully completed sale is 12% of the agreed asset sale price. It is made up of the following components:

2%Administration FeeMarketplace administration and operational management.
5%Marketplace Service FeeListing, transaction workflow and marketplace support services.
5%Payment & Wallet Operations FeePayment coordination, transaction records and wallet/payout operations.
Total Nexa marketplace fee12%

Who pays the fee?

The 12% Nexa fee is deducted from the seller's proceeds after a completed sale. The seller's Asking Price remains unchanged and is not automatically increased by the fee. The buyer is not charged a separate Nexa platform fee on top of the displayed agreed purchase price.

Example calculation

If an asset sells for USD 1,000, the Nexa fee is USD 120 (2% = USD 20 administration, 5% = USD 50 marketplace service, and 5% = USD 50 payment and wallet operations). The seller's balance before any separate third-party charges, taxes or required deductions is USD 880. The buyer's agreed purchase price remains USD 1,000.

Payment providers, currencies and wallet balances

Available payment methods and currencies depend on the buyer's and seller's country, the transaction and provider availability. Third-party processor, bank, wire-transfer, foreign-exchange or payout charges are not part of the 12% Nexa fee and may apply separately. Any such known charge must be disclosed before the affected party commits or payout is initiated. Seller proceeds may appear as pending while payment verification, asset handover, buyer confirmation, dispute checks or provider settlement is outstanding. Funds become available for withdrawal only under the applicable transaction and provider rules.

Nexa does not promise instant settlement, guaranteed exchange rates or guaranteed withdrawal timing. Users should use only payment instructions displayed through the official Nexa transaction flow and keep their payment references.

If the asset is not delivered or materially differs from the agreed description, report the issue promptly with supporting evidence. Sellers must have transferable rights, provide truthful information, disclose material restrictions and respond to reasonable verification requests. The 12% Nexa marketplace fee is deducted from completed-sale proceeds as described above.
