Nexa Policies

These policy outlines explain the principles Nexa intends to follow. They are working drafts, not final contractual terms. They must be completed against the actual operating model, applicable law and payment-provider arrangements before being treated as binding policies.

Terms & Conditions

Users must provide accurate information, protect their account credentials and use Nexa lawfully. Sellers must have the rights and authority needed to offer each listed asset and must not misrepresent ownership, traffic, audience, revenue or other material facts. Buyers should review listing information and agree transfer requirements before paying.

Nexa may review listings, request verification, restrict content or suspend accounts where there are reasonable concerns about fraud, unlawful activity, inaccurate information or policy violations. The final terms must define eligibility, user responsibilities, transaction completion, complaints, termination, liability, governing law and dispute handling.

Privacy Policy

Nexa may process account details, contact information, identity and address verification submissions, listing information, transaction records, device/security data and support communications where needed to operate and protect the marketplace.

The final policy must identify the legal entity responsible for data, lawful purposes, retention periods, processors, international transfers, user rights, security practices and the contact point for privacy requests. Sensitive verification documents should be access-restricted and retained only as legally and operationally necessary.

Cookie Policy

The website may use essential cookies or similar technologies for sign-in sessions, security and core functionality. Any analytics or advertising technologies should be disclosed and, where required, used only after the user has provided the appropriate consent.

Payment & Fee Policy

Applicable platform fees and payment-provider charges must be shown clearly before a user commits to a transaction. Payment methods may vary by country and currency. Users should pay only to the official payment destination displayed by Nexa and should retain their transaction reference.

Seller funds may be recorded as pending after payment verification. Release to an available balance must follow the actual buyer-confirmation, dispute and payment-provider process. Nexa must not describe its flow as regulated escrow or guaranteed payment protection unless the legal and provider arrangements genuinely support that claim.

Buyer Protection

Buyers should receive clear asset details, seller information and transfer requirements before purchase. The final policy must specify evidence requirements, confirmation windows, reporting channels and the steps available if an asset is not delivered or materially differs from the agreed description.

Seller Policy

Sellers must own or be authorized to transfer the listed asset, provide truthful supporting information, disclose material restrictions and cooperate with reasonable verification and transfer checks. Stolen, compromised, misleading, prohibited or rights-infringing assets must not be listed.

Refund Policy

Refund eligibility must be assessed against the transaction record, asset-transfer status, evidence supplied by both parties, applicable law and payment-provider rules. The final policy must state the specific grounds, process, time limits and any non-refundable charges before purchases are enabled at scale.

Disputes & Complaints

Users should raise transaction disputes through the official support channel and provide relevant order references and evidence. Nexa should record the complaint, give both parties a fair opportunity to respond and communicate the outcome and any available next steps.

Important: These are intentionally preliminary policy summaries. Nexa should obtain qualified legal review and confirm its registered business details, payment-provider contracts, fee schedule, refund workflow and governing jurisdiction before publishing final terms or accepting real-money transactions.
